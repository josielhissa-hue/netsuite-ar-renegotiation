# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-12

- Calculation engine: penalty, simple/compound late interest, discount tiers, Price/SAC/Flat schedules in integer cents, integrity fingerprint.
- Simulation wizard (Suitelet) with approve/reject endpoint.
- User Event controls: segregation of duties, approval limits by role, locked terms, status transitions, delete protection.
- Map/Reduce execution: balance drift check, credit memo, charges journal, down payment invoice, idempotent re-runs.
- Scheduled monitor: installment billing, overdue and breach detection, cure, settlement, execution retry.
- Simulation RESTlet.
- SDF objects, documentation and 18 unit tests.
